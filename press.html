Patrick Salvado Idringi is an award-winning Ugandan stand-up comedian, host and actor, known on stage as the Man from Ombokolo. A former telecoms engineer, he turned to comedy full time in 2011. He is the creator of Africa Laughs, one of East Africa's biggest comedy showcases, and has performed across Africa, the Gulf and the UK.

Long · about 170 words

Patrick Salvado Idringi is one of Uganda's best-known comedians. Born in Kampala in 1985, with family roots in Koboko in the West Nile, he studied telecommunications engineering at Makerere University and worked as a switch engineer at MTN Uganda before finishing as first runner-up in Standup Uganda in 2009.

He went full time in 2011 and built his reputation with the sold-out one-man show Man from Ombokolo, winning Best Male Comedian at the 2015 Uganda Entertainment Awards. He reached the semi-finals of the World's Funniest Person competition in 2016 and was twice nominated for Pan African Comic of the Year at the Savannah Comic Choice Awards.

Salvado created Africa Laughs, which has hosted Basketmouth, Eric Omondi, Celeste Ntuli and Mpho Popps among others, and has performed in 13 countries including Nigeria, South Africa, the UAE and the UK. He has hosted breakfast radio on Capital FM and Sanyu FM, his own DStv series The Salvado Show, and voiced a character in Disney+'s Kizazi Moto: Generation Fire. Off stage, he founded the Dawa Foundation for children from less privileged homes and is a WildAid ambassador.
