About

A telecoms engineer from Kampala who became one of the best-known names in African comedy.

Home / About

Serious money from being unserious

Patrick Salvado Idringi was born in Kampala on 14 February 1985, one of eight children. His father's family comes from Koboko in Uganda's West Nile region, and that northern heritage runs through his comedy. Ombokolo, the village at the heart of his stage persona, is where his stories begin.

He studied telecommunications engineering at Makerere University and worked as a switch engineer at MTN Uganda. Encouraged by his sisters, he entered the Standup Uganda competition in 2009 and finished second. Two years later he left engineering for comedy full time.

His one-man show Man from Ombokolo sold out the Kampala Serena Hotel in 2015, and his concert Africa Laughs has grown into one of East Africa's biggest comedy showcases, with headliners such as Basketmouth, Eric Omondi, Celeste Ntuli and Mpho Popps. His style is observational, deadpan and quick, with a dark edge.

Career

Fifteen years on stage

  1. 2008

    Engineer first

    Graduates from Makerere University with a BSc in Telecommunications Engineering and works as a switch engineer at MTN Uganda.

  2. 2009

    The first stage

    First runner-up in Standup Uganda, the MultiChoice comedy competition. Co-founds the comedy group The Crackers.

  3. 2011

    Comedy full time

    Leaves engineering for comedy and joins 91.3 Capital FM as a radio presenter.

  4. 2013

    Africa Laughs begins

    Launches Africa Laughs, his comedy concert bringing the continent's top comedians to Kampala.

  5. 2015

    Man from Ombokolo

    His first one-man show sells out the Kampala Serena Hotel. Wins Best Male Comedian at the Uganda Entertainment Awards.

  6. 2016

    Going global

    Reaches the semi-finals of the World's Funniest Person competition and performs at the Apollo Theatre in London.

  7. 2017

    Building more

    Man from Ombokolo 2. Becomes a WildAid ambassador and founds the Dawa Foundation and the events company Sheer Solutions Africa.

  8. 2020

    Breakfast radio

    Hosts the 88.2 Sanyu FM Breakfast Show until 2022.

  9. 2022

    The Salvado Show

    Launches his own celebrity talk and comedy show on Pearl Magic Prime on DStv.

  10. 2023

    Disney+

    Voices Dushiime in the animated anthology Kizazi Moto: Generation Fire.

  11. 2025

    Fifteen years

    Celebrates 15 years in comedy, plays the Laugh Africa Comedy Festival in Johannesburg and hosts Africa Laughs Season 7.
