Questions
Does Salvado perform outside Uganda?

Yes. He has performed in 13 countries, including Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, Botswana, Malawi, Mauritius, the UAE, Qatar and the UK. Travel and accommodation are agreed as part of the booking.

How far in advance should we book?

Four to eight weeks is ideal, and longer for international dates. December and the wedding season fill up quickly.

Can the set be kept clean for a corporate audience?

Yes. Corporate and family sets are kept clean, and material can be tailored to your organisation or event.

Can he host our event as well as perform?

Yes. With years of breakfast radio and his own TV show behind him, Salvado regularly hosts launches, award ceremonies and concerts.

What do we need to provide on the day?

A good sound system with a wireless handheld microphone, stage lighting and a stool. The full list is on the press page.
