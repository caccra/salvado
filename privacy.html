Privacy

How this website collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Home / Privacy policy
Last updated

13 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Patrick Salvado Idringi and his management team in Kampala, Uganda. Contact us about privacy at salvadopi@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Booking and media enquiries: your name, email address and any event details you choose to share in the booking form.
  • Mailing list: your email address and, if you give it, your nearest city, so we can tell you about shows near you.
  • Website analytics: anonymous, aggregated visit statistics such as pages viewed, country and device type. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your enquiry, to send show announcements you asked for, and to understand how the website is used so we can improve it. We never sell your information.

Who processes it

Form submissions and mailing list sign-ups may be handled by trusted service providers acting on our behalf, such as our form and email providers and our hosting provider, Vercel. They may store data outside Uganda, including in the United States and the European Union, under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to handle the booking and meet our legal obligations. You stay on the mailing list until you unsubscribe, which you can do at any time using the link in every email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and the UK and EU GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email salvadopi@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Embedded content

Some pages link to or embed content from YouTube and X (Twitter). Those services may collect data under their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed.
